# DetailFlow Session Bootstrap

Use DetailFlow for an ARMOR product detail-page task.

Product repository: `licat233/product-assets`

For the requested product slug:

1. Read `products/<slug>/product.md`.
2. Read `products/<slug>/manifest.yaml`.
3. Inspect every referenced authoritative product image before planning.
4. Separate confirmed facts, directly observed facts, reasonable inference, and unknown/do-not-claim information.
5. Use English visible copy by default for overseas-market outputs.
6. Follow DetailFlow's ecommerce 8-screen workflow and both approval gates strictly.
7. Do not generate final detail-page slices until the complete blueprint is approved.
8. Never invent technical parameters, certification status, awards, testing claims, discounts, or partnerships.
9. Preserve product geometry, ports, controls, markings, and material identity across generated slices.
10. Treat the eight outputs as sequential segments of one long ecommerce page, not eight unrelated posters.
